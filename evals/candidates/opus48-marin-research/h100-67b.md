# Bringing the 67B-A2B up on H100s

**Short answer.** The 67B-A2B (the d2560 Grug MoE, 67.1B total / 2.01B active) was *originally* the June hero run and was supposed to launch on CoreWeave H100s — the June milestone was literally titled *"June: kick off overtrained 67B-A2B 10T on H100s."* The bring-up stalled: the full-depth production shape hit a hard first-step memory wall, and the Muon optimizer plus the parallelism options to hide it were too expensive on H100 interconnect. GPU MoE MFU on the relevant shape topped out at **~19.9% MFU on four nodes (SGD/Adam)** and never cleared its 20% bar, so the run was **moved to TPU v4-2048** and launched there. H100 work then continued as a *validation / upskilling* track on smaller shapes, where the numbers got better (~23.8%, and later ~26.5% weak-scaled), but the 67B-A2B itself did not train on H100s.

---

## What we were trying to do, and the strategic frame

The whole H100 effort was a de-risking question, not just an ops task: *"whether Marin/Grug can reach acceptable MoE throughput on the relevant NVIDIA GPU shapes, or whether we need to move the training stack to Megatron/TE"* ([#4302](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4302)). The decision was to **stay on JAX/Grug/Levanter and close the gap with native kernels** rather than migrate — dlwh: *"hoping we don't have to switch to megatron!"* — using FA4/CuTe attention, Triton/SonicMoE grouped-GEMM, and expert-parallel transport as the JAX-native answers to a Megatron-Core+TE reference ([#4302 comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4302#issuecomment-4727421242)). The concrete June goal was tracked in epic [#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052) ("Make sure 6/15 H100 cluster works and we can train our June run on it") against the CoreWeave US-EAST-02A H100 pool.

## What worked

- **Cluster + stack bring-up.** The H100 cluster crossed from booting to actually running Grug MoE jobs. Operability fixes landed: staging the CUDA 13 toolchain so JAX/Pallas Mosaic kernels compile on CoreWeave workers (#6637), a default JAX compile cache (#6640), Levanter caches/checkpoints to CoreWeave S3 (#6676/#6677/#6548), and a W&B `finish()` hang fix (#6659) — narrated in [June 22-28 summary / #6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052).
- **Kernel milestones.** A DeepEP-free Pallas/Mosaic MGPU **fused MoE kernel** (#6597, tracked under #6694) was pushed through H100 correctness milestones with real speedups (the fused down-unpermute stage fell from ~40 ms to ~2.6 ms; ~2.09× over the ragged all-to-all baseline), the D128 GQA FA4 backward was routed through native SM90 (#6613), and a B-tiled fused cross-entropy bounded H100 logit memory (#6572) — all in [#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052).
- **A per-device roofline/attribution dashboard** for the d2560 shape (#6557/#6573) that made the optimizer cost measurable ([#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052)).

## What didn't work (the blockers)

- **First-step memory wall.** Dozens of MAY-0xx diagnostic runs localized a deterministic pre-step OOM: full-depth d2560 / 26-layer, seq-4096 runs reliably requested **~63-74 GiB before step 0**, un-rescuable by fp32-vs-bf16 master params, Pallas-vs-XLA cross-entropy, or even `recompute_all` remat (recompute_all only got to ~91 GiB before OOMing on a 27 GiB request). A seq-2048 variant of the same shape fit and a one-layer seq-4096 fit — pinning it to full-depth activation/remat liveness ([#6367](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367), via [June 15-21 summary / #6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052)).
- **Muon is too expensive on H100.** Profiling the single-node MuonH step (#6493) found **MuonH at 1.49 s/step (~22K tok/s, ~4.75% MFU) vs an SGD A/B at 0.44 s/step (~75K tok/s, ~16.2% MFU)** on the identical shape — ~1.05 s/step of pure Muon overhead. On a two-node run, David Hall measured Muon's Newton-Schulz work at **~500 ms of a 1,500 ms step**, and under FSDP Russell Power quantified the Muon tax at **+0.92 s/step on two nodes (~60% throughput hit)** that grows as the gradient all-gather crosses the inter-node link. The single-node grouping experiment ([#6493](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6493)) returned a negative result (grouped/padded Newton-Schulz neither avoided autotune OOM nor improved throughput). **Verdict: shelve Muon on GPU** — a ~10% step-count gain isn't worth the cost at this interconnect ([#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052)).
- **Pipeline parallelism couldn't rescue it either.** Russell Power's 1F1B / zero-bubble PP prototype (#6532, PR #6534) was gradient-exact and did unlock memory scaling (fit an 8M-token batch where FSDP OOMs, and amortized Muon ~3.2× better than FSDP), but it **ran ~0.78× of FSDP on a single host** and only pulled ahead (~1.22×) once FSDP's all-gather crossed a slower interconnect at v6e-32. He *"could not beat FSDP with 1F1B or zero-bubble on H100,"* so **PR #6534 was closed unmerged** and parked to an external repo ([#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052)).
- **DeepEP transport didn't fit our process model.** An assignment-native DeepEP path recovered some gap on EP16 (~19.7 MFU vs ~16) but Marin's one-JAX-process-per-8-GPU-node model is fundamentally incompatible with DeepEP normal-mode's one-process-per-GPU buffer ownership ([June 15-21 summary](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052)).

**The load-bearing "final MFU" for the 67B-A2B-on-H100 attempt itself is ~19.9% on four nodes (SGD/Adam), which never cleared 20%** — after which the run left H100s. The higher 23.8% and 26.5% numbers are **not the 67B-A2B**: 23.8% is the smaller d2048 / 11B-A1.5B validation model, and 26.5% is a full-model *config* with the expert count dropped to 64 in a weak-scaling probe. Don't read either as the 67B-A2B hitting that on H100.

## How it resolved: the run went to TPU

The June 67B-A2B run **did start — but on TPU, not H100.** After the H100 bring-up stalled, Larry Dial staged the d2560 shape onto TPU v4 (where XLA handles Muon's Newton-Schulz cheaply), starting on a v4-1024 the morning of **June 24** and settling on the full **v4-2048 (1,024 chips) in us-central2 by June 27**, as `moe_67b_a2b_d2560_ep1_rep16_bs4096_seq8192_sw2k_v4_2048_muon_10T` running MuonH on the May Recipe ([#6044](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6044)). hammer confirmed the pivot: *"you mentioned we have changed plans for this run and now plan to run on TPUs instead of GPUs"* ([#6044 comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6044#issuecomment-4787928368)). On TPU the same shape got good Muon throughput; early v4 runs measured ~21.5% MFU / ~78-day 10T projection at EP=1 ([#6044 comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6044#issuecomment-4805351035)). By July it was ~2.3T tokens in on v4-2048 with a clean 2T intermediate cooldown at 2.277 Paloma ([July 6-12 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-07-06_2026-07-12.html)). The milestone name itself dropped "on H100s" between the June-15 and later framings.

The H100 track continued for a different purpose — a **hardware/loss validation POC** (epic [#6716](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6716), the ~23.8% d2048 run) and a **GPU Upskilling Master Plan** ([#6998](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6998), Learning Path #6979) targeting an XXB-A2B on Hopper at 20-25% MFU on 128-256 H100s with pipeline parallelism as the real fix — ahead of the September Blackwell run, not to re-home the 67B-A2B.

### Gaps / caveats
- Direct **67B-A2B (d2560, 64-expert) full-model H100 training attempts** do exist — Larry Dial's `dial-h100-fullmodelpy-d2560-e64-muonh-sonic-...` runs on 2026-07-07 — but every one is logged `state=failed` ([wandb](https://wandb.ai/marin-community/marin_moe/runs/dial-h100-fullmodelpy-d2560-e64-muonh-sonic-b128-seq4k-20260707-022829)), so there is no clean full-shape H100 training MFU for the 67B-A2B; the ~19.9% four-node number on the proxy shape is the closest thing to a "final" figure.
- A separate v0 of **67B-A2B *inference* on GPUs** landed in main on 2026-07-14 (`test_june_67b_a2b_vllm_s3_inference.py`, related to #7116/#7117), with an early 8×H100 BF16 export run hitting an NCCL AllToAll error — that's serving, not the training bring-up this question is about ([#inference](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1385733711013871729/1526388201915289642)).
