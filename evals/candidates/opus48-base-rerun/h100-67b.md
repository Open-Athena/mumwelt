# Bringing the 67B-A2B up on H100s

## The short version

We got the 67B-A2B shape **profiled and optimized on H100s at small node counts, but never running at scale** — and it ultimately went to TPU instead.

The shape in question is the d2560 "May Recipe" Grug MoE: 26 layers, 256 experts top-4, seq 4096, `parameter_count=67078882816` — 67.1B total / 2.01B active per token. Two things happened in parallel through June 2026:

1. **At 1–4 nodes it worked, and MFU climbed from ~4.75% to ~19.9%** — mostly by removing Muon and fixing a cross-entropy memory blowup.
2. **At 128–256 GPUs it never started.** [#6367 "Experiment: Grug MoE d2560 H100 MFU"](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367), opened 2026-06-14 targeting "a repeatable profile-bearing short run at >=20% training MFU," died on a first-step memory wall. Its own Results section says, verbatim and twice: **"No >=20 MFU claim yet."** No run under #6367 ever produced an MFU number at all.

**The final MFU for the 67B-A2B on H100 is ~19.9%, on 4 nodes (32 H100) with SGD/Adam**, reported by dlwh out of the June 26 OA meeting ([`#gpu`, 2026-06-26](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1399998407657001062/1520200189690773564)):

> "We're currently at **~19.9 MFU on 4 nodes w/ SGD/Adam**. Some low hanging fruit remains. **Muon needs to go for now.** 10% step-count improvement isn't worth atm. **We'll need much faster interconnect or PP.**"

That is just under the stated bar, and the bar was set at a scale never reached: [#6693](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6693) asks for ">20 MFU at 128 GPU scale." Meanwhile the run itself left. Per the [2026-06-22_2026-06-28 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-06-22_2026-06-28.html):

> "After three weeks stalled on CoreWeave's H100s behind a first-step memory wall and Muon's poor fit under Fully Sharded Data Parallel (FSDP), the 67B-A2B production shape (the d2560 Grug MoE, 67.1B total / 2.01B active per token) was staged onto TPU instead, where XLA absorbs Muon's Newton-Schulz cost."

Larry Dial started on a v4-1024 the morning of June 24 and settled on the full v4-2048 by June 27 ([#6044](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6044): "We have started the run on TPUs this morning(v4-1024)").

The dominant single lever was **removing Muon**: 4.75% → 16.09% on the same node. The second was the **cross-entropy fix**: 9.51% → ~13%.

For contrast, the same d2560 shape on the hardware it actually ran on — **TPU v4-1024, EP=1, BS=4096: 1,485,929 tok/s at 21.5% MFU, 22.6 s/step** ([#6044](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6044#issuecomment-4805561164)). On the full v4-2048 the team accepted **~13–18.6%** per-chip MFU for absolute throughput, to cut projected wall-clock.

## What we tried

### 1. Cluster bring-up: correctness worked, throughput did not (#6292 / #6293, June 9–10)

The `cw-us-east-02a` ladder (1 node → 64 H100 → 256 H100) succeeded on its own terms. PR [#6293](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6293) added `expert_axis_size`/`replica_axis_size` to `GrugTrainerConfig`, giving mesh `(replica_dcn=1, data=32, expert=8, model=1)` — FSDP over the cross-node `data` axis (InfiniBand), expert parallelism over the intra-node `expert` axis (NVLink). A 89.67B-total / 5.34B-active MoE ran "50/50 steps, loss decreasing (→ 6.93), 218K tokens/sec, zero OOM / zero sharding errors" on 256 H100 ([#6292](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6292)). The verdict on that run was blunt: **"MFU is low (~3%)."**

### 2. The 256-GPU MFU campaign (#6304) — 2.8% → 9.37%, on a *different* shape

[#6304](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6304) targeted "≥25% active-FLOPs MFU on 256 H100 (≈2.0M tok/s) at the 90B-5.3BA shape." **Note this is the 90B-A5.3B shape, not the 67B-A2B** — do not read 9.37% as a 67B number. The baseline profile explained everything:

> communication (NCCL) **73.4%** / stall 18.9% / compute **7.7%**
> `AllGather_RING_LL`: 37,304 launches in 8 steps ≈ **583 all-gathers per GPU per step**, avg 2.0 ms each

What **worked**: raising global batch (256 → 512 → 1024 gave 2.8% → 5.37% → 9.32%) and a `save_moe` remat setting (712K tok/s, **9.37%**, best). What **failed**: an XLA collective-combine + latency-hiding flag bundle (−7%, compile time 14→25 min); batch 1536/2048 (OOM); seq 4096 × batch 512 (OOM, 59.18 GiB); PGLE (OOM + "PGLE collected an empty trace"); pure-bf16 params (a **deterministic gang hang** — "32 ranks silent mid-collective 12+ min… No NCCL warnings, no OOM, no crash — a clean deadlock"); and microbatching, which fits batch 1536 but drops MFU to 7.18%, because "FSDP + microbatching re-gathers the sharded params on every microbatch."

The OOM ceiling turned out to be the **token-embedding backward**, not MoE dispatch: GSPMD cannot all-to-all reshard batch-sharded(256) → hidden-sharded(32) with mismatched tile counts, so it replicates the full cotangent — "3 GiB/device at batch 1024, 18 GiB at 1536" ([#6304](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6304#issuecomment-4671478500)). The principled fix was correct (SPMD warnings 2→0, bit-identical loss) and *still lost* — −11% throughput, OOM'd earlier — so it was reverted.

**Close-out** ([2026-06-10](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6304#issuecomment-4674696103)): "**2.8% → 9.37% MFU** (3.3×)… **~9.4% is the practical pure-FSDP ceiling for this 90B/5.3BA model on 256 H100**." Deferred levers tracked in [#6321](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6321).

### 3. The 67B shape at scale (#6367, June 14–24) — the first-step memory wall

This is where the 67B-A2B itself was pushed to 128–256 GPUs, and it never reached a train step cleanly:

- **OOM before step 0.** `GM2560-MFU-004` "reached train-loop startup but failed before step 0 with `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED: Out of memory while trying to allocate 60.91GiB` on multiple ranks" ([comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367#issuecomment-4700966118)).
- **Loss materialization was the choke point.** MAY-055 (B8/N4, seq4096) "OOMed on first loss materialization with `132.44GiB`"; MAY-058 at **seq 1024** still "OOMed at `jax.block_until_ready(metrics["train/loss"])` with `63.80GiB`"; MAY-059 with patched AdamH reproduced it at `63.69GiB`.
- **Collective startup stalls.** Runs at 128 and 256 devices "repeatedly emitted pre-step `Initialize clique: devices=128` rendezvous warnings, with zero W&B scalar history."
- **Silent saturation.** Multiple runs sat "around 81.0 GiB/H100 with 0% GPU util" for hours, pre-step. At B16/N2, "XLA remat could only reduce from 91.96 GiB to 90.93 GiB and then OOMed on a 27.06 GiB allocation."
- **A counterintuitive inversion.** Cutting global batch was *not* the lever — cutting the data axis removed FSDP sharding and *raised* the memory floor. "preserving `data=16` was the key."

Tried against it: FA4/CuTe attention; an opt-in bf16-live/fp32-master parameter mode; `XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_MEM_FRACTION=0.95`; replicated input/output embeddings; `remat=recompute_all`; Pallas cross-entropy; a routed-expert 3D AdamH; one-axis probes down to B8/N1; topology-preserving minimizers.

The **one usable full-sequence profile**, MAY-042 (B128 on 16 nodes / 128 GPUs, seq 4096, `data=16`/`expert=8`/`model=1`, ring MoE) reached step 3, completed 6/6 steps at loss 11.3, and showed **compute 65.65% / comm 33.68% / stall 0.68%** — a far better balance than the 90B run. But dlwh was explicit ([comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367#issuecomment-4706090648)):

> "The profile did not recover steady-state step markers, so **I am not claiming MFU from this run.**"

The headline verdict comment ([2026-06-15](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367#issuecomment-4710624468)): "attention/MoE throughput work has a viable baseline, but **the immediate blocker is still a first-step memory allocation around the loss/output/vocab or SPMD liveness path**." A 128-GPU full profile (MAY-060) was launched 2026-06-16 and **no result was ever posted**. The last comment on the thread is FA4/CuTe kernel work on 2026-06-24.

### 4. Two structural bets tried and set aside

**Muon — shelved on GPU.** David Hall's roofline dashboard [#6573](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6573) measured Muon eating "roughly 500 ms of a 1,500 ms H100 step," so the June 26 OA meeting dropped it: a ~10% step-count gain doesn't pay for the interconnect cost. Clean hardware split — TPU keeps Muon, GPU drops it. This was worth ~11 MFU points on a single node (4.75% → 16.09%).

**Pipeline parallelism — failed, then revived.** Russell Power, 2026-06-26: "i wasn't able to get great results out of PP with 1f1B or zero-bubble ([#6532](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6532))… i couldn't beat FSDP." PR [#6534](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6534) closed unmerged. It returned in July via JaxPP.

None of these was the fundamental blocker, but all cost time:

## What worked

The kernel and compute-lane work delivered even where the at-scale run didn't:

- **B-tiled fused cross-entropy** ([#6572](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6572), tracked [#6596](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6596)) — routes H100 BF16 large-vocab CE through a batch-tiled path with an 8192-row tile, bounding peak logits memory. Directly worth **9.51% → ~13% MFU** on the d2560 shape ([#6695](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6695#issuecomment-4823466041)).
- **FA4/CuTe SM90 attention backward** for D128 GQA ([#6613](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6613), closing [#6632](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6632)) — backward **1.10 ms → 0.5229 ms** (14.8% → 31.15% speed-of-light), 96.4% of upstream-local. Narrow routing: H100/SM90 native path only for GQA with `head_dim=128`.
- **Pallas/Mosaic MGPU fused MoE kernel** ([#6597](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6597), under [#6694](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6694)) — the chosen forward bet after Muon and PP were set aside. ~**2.09×** over the ragged all-to-all baseline by late June, fused down-unpermute falling ~40 ms → ~2 ms. By early July: "**218 TFLOP/s per rank, about 13.8% faster** than the serial ring-prologue baseline, and a one-node 20-step trainer smoke ran the path end to end at **about 20% MFU and 554k tokens/s** — right at the commitment's bar, on Hopper" ([#6841](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6841), [#6848](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6848)).
- **fp8 on Hopper**, Matt Wittmann end to end: a merged haliax `Fp8DirectDotGeneralOp` feeding genuine E4M3 operands ([#6660](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6660)); an H100 fp8 ragged-dot kernel at **1.41×** vs tuned bf16 ([#6880](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6880), preregistered at a ≥20% bar in [#6824](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6824)); **1.438×** end-to-end with fp8 on the dispatch and combine wires ([#6911](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6911)). Defaults stay bf16 pending loss-curve validation ([#6699](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6699)).
- **Fleet scale-up** — Russell Power tripled the H100 fleet with the 512-GPU RNO2A cluster ([#6909](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6909)).
- **Early baseline sanity** — on 2 GH200s multinode, Marin was "8% faster than Megatron out of the box; 18% if include all the bells/whistles" (percyliang, [#5356](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/5356), 2026-05-18).

## What didn't

- **Getting the 67B shape to start at 128–256 GPUs.** The first-step loss/vocab allocation (~63.7 GiB, recurring even at seq 1024) was never root-caused; the suspected globally-replicated logits buffer surviving SPMD lowering is a hypothesis, not a finding.
- **Muon on GPU** — ~500 ms of a 1,500 ms step, shelved.
- **Pipeline parallelism, first attempt** — 1F1B and zero-bubble both lost to FSDP; PR closed unmerged.
- **Pure-FSDP at 256 GPUs** — 73–82% communication, latency-bound on ~583 all-gathers per GPU per step, and simultaneously memory-capped at the batch that would amortize them. Ceiling ~9.4% for the 90B shape.
- **The ≥20% MFU bar at 128 GPUs** — never met on H100 in this corpus window.

## Where it stands now (as of 2026-07-12/15)

The H100 track was de-prioritized in late June but **not abandoned**. #6367 was effectively superseded on 2026-06-26 by the [#6693](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6693) workstream tracker, with d2560 work handed off to [#4312](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4312) (single-node profiling) and [#6493](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6493) (Muon).

The unblock came from pipeline parallelism returning via JaxPP — David Hall stood up JaxPP PP MoE training across four 8×H100 nodes on RNO2A ([#7024](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7024)), pivoting to an explicit `jaxpp.experimental.mpmd` path after the automatic schedule stayed blocked on JaxPP sharding inference. His verdict in `#gpu` on [2026-07-10](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1399998407657001062/1525217063830949888): **"Hopper is back on the menu I think."** A related concurrent-QuACK blocker ([#7110](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7110)) was fixed with a per-handler mutex validated at 32-H100 scale, "though at 13.96 MFU it is not itself competitive with ring EP."

Separately, a **smaller** proxy did clear a good number in July: [#6716](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6716) brought an **11B-A1.5B** model (d2048/L24, 64 experts top-4, GQA 4:1, 1.53B active / 10.6B total) up on **64×H100**, holding **~23.8% MFU** (~1.37M tok/s, ~1.53 s/step at batch 512 × seq 4096) over a 500B-token run. Two caveats before quoting that as "the H100 result": it is **~6× smaller than the 67B-A2B**, and it was explicitly a shakedown — Isaac Hodes: "this is more about exercising the hardware instead of delivering an artifact." Also, "the FLOP counter treats attention as fully quadratic and ignores the 2048-token sliding window, so the reported MFU is slightly optimistic."

Relatedly, Larry Dial's GPU MFU Learning Path ([#6979](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6979)) found the full trainable MoE `model.py` **weak-scales essentially flat, 26.7% at 8 GPUs to 26.5% at 64 H100s** at global batch 1024, because replica/DDP hides the once-per-step gradient all-reduce over the data-center network while keeping frequent FSDP all-gathers on intra-node NVLink; sharding the model across nodes costs about 4 points. Dropping total experts 256 → 64 (still top-4) lets the full model fit one node, proposed as the H100 working copy.

H100s' role has changed regardless. They are no longer a candidate for the flagship pretraining run — that is TPU v4-2048 today (67B-A2B, ~2.3T tokens in, completion targeted ~Aug 30) and B200/NVL72 from end of July. H100s now serve as a GPU-stack validation surface, an **inference** surface for 67B-A2B checkpoints ([#7116](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/7116) added an 8×H100 BF16 inference regression on the step-18000 checkpoint; Romain Yon shipped "a v0 of Grug 67b a2b inference on GPUs" to main on 2026-07-14, though a rerun of the assertion in [#7117](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/7117) **hit an NCCL AlltoAll error**), and an upskilling target in its own right — [#6998](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6998) sets a **target** of 20–25% MFU on 128–256 H100s.

## Gaps and caveats

- **#6367 has no close event, closing comment, or state field in the corpus.** Its last activity is 2026-06-24. I report it as stalled-and-superseded, which is what the weekly summaries assert, not as formally closed.
- **[#6998](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6998), [#6979](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6979) and [#7024](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7024) post-date the frozen GitHub corpus** and are known only through weekly-summary prose and Discord. The 26.5%@64-H100, 13.96%@32-H100 and 20–25% target figures inherit that weaker sourcing.
- [#6716](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6716) is a one-line stub in the corpus; the ~23.8% figure comes from the [2026-07-06_2026-07-12 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-07-06_2026-07-12.html), not the issue thread. The run was due to finish July 13–14; no completion report exists in-corpus.
- **The ~63.7 GiB first-step allocation that killed the at-scale d2560 runs was never root-caused** in any source I could find.
- The single-node d2560 numbers (4.75% / 16.09% / ~14.8% ceiling) come from sibling threads [#6493](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6493), [#4312](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4312) and [#6695](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6695) rather than #6367 itself, which is why they are easy to miss when reading the headline experiment thread alone.
