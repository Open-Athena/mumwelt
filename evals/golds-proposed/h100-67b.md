# Bringing the 67B-A2B up on H100s

**Short answer.** The 67B-A2B (the d2560 / 26-layer / 256-expert-top-4 Grug MoE, 67.1B total / 2.01B active — the "May Recipe") was the June hero run and was supposed to launch on CoreWeave's US-EAST-02A H100s: the June milestone was literally *"June: kick off overtrained 67B-A2B 10T on H100s"* ([June 15-21 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-06-15_2026-06-21.html)). Bring-up stalled on two walls — a deterministic first-step OOM on the full-depth production shape, and the Muon optimizer (plus every parallelism option tried to hide it) being too expensive on H100 interconnect. GPU MoE MFU on the production shape topped out at **~19.9% MFU (achieved) on four nodes = 32 H100 with SGD/Adam** and never cleared its 20% bar ([#6693](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6693)), so the hero run was **moved off GPU onto TPU** and launched there — where it is now training on **v4-2048 at ~18.6% sustained MFU (achieved)**. H100 work then continued, and by July was explicitly **re-scoped and revived** as an upskilling / Blackwell-prep push (fused expert-parallel kernel + JaxPP pipeline parallelism, *"Hopper is back on the menu"*), reaching a best measured **JaxPP point of 18.26% mean MFU on 32 H100 (achieved, ~1.74 pts below the 20% bar)** and a flat weak-scaling **26.7% → 26.5% MFU (8 → 64 H100)** on a reduced 64-expert working copy — but the actual 67B/256-expert shape never trained on H100.

---

## 1. What we were trying to do (the strategic frame)

The H100 effort was a **de-risking question**, not just ops: the parent issue [#4302](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4302) ("H100 x 8 MOE MFU perf") framed the goal as *"Derisk whether Marin/Grug can reach acceptable MoE throughput on the relevant NVIDIA GPU shapes, or whether we need to move the training stack to Megatron/TE or another platform,"* with a near-term target of within ~10% of the best Megatron/TE BF16 B200 result. The tie-breaking preference was to stay JAX-native and close the gap with kernels rather than migrate — dlwh, in the body of the sibling issue [#4312](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4312) ("Improve end-to-end MFU of MOE on 8xH100 node"): *"Hoping we don't have to switch to megatron!"* (Note: that quote is #4312's opener, not a #4302 comment.) The concrete >20% MFU workstream was tracked in [#6693](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6693) ("[tracking] >20 MFU at 128 GPUs workstream plan"); the June cluster-readiness epic was [#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052) against the CoreWeave US-EAST-02A H100 pool; the production-shape MFU experiment was [#6367](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367).

## 2. What worked

- **Cluster + stack bring-up.** The CoreWeave US-EAST-02A H100 cluster came live with green canaries and ran real Grug MoE jobs ([#6052](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6052); [June 15-21 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-06-15_2026-06-21.html)).
- **A per-device roofline/attribution profile** that made the cost structure measurable — `jax.named_scope` regions around the router/MoE internals, giving a clean forward/backward attribution ([#4312](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4312), see §5).
- **FA4/CuTe Hopper attention and a DeepEP-free fused MoE kernel.** A Pallas Mosaic-GPU (MGPU) fused MoE kernel path passed H100 correctness/parity smokes and a one-node 20-step trainer smoke at **~20% MFU (achieved, single-node 8×H100)** on `/dlwh/grug-moe-pallas-mgpu-20step-smoke` ([#6597](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6597), June 29). This kernel — extended to overlap the all-gathers on NVLink — became the July "real fix" vehicle (§7).
- **Muon *kernel* optimization in isolation.** Grouped/stacked, then a 4D `[group, expert, m, n]` Newton-Schulz layout got the full-production MuonH update kernel at the real 26-layer shape to **~50% of nominal 8×H100 bf16 peak with zero hidden collectives, ~1.55× faster than the prior H5 path** ([#6493](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6493); [June 15-21 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-06-15_2026-06-21.html)). This was the *compute* win; the *sharding/comms* cost is what still killed it (§3).
- **DeepEP internode transport, proven viable under a different process model.** Upstream DeepEP normal-mode assumes one process per GPU; Marin runs one JAX process per 8-GPU host. The mismatch was **worked around** — under a supervised process-per-GPU shape (2 Iris tasks of H100×8, each spawning 8 single-GPU workers), internode dispatch and combine (forward and backward VJP) smokes **passed** on CoreWeave, and the native NVSHMEM/IBGDA path itself was proven (correct bootstrap iface `enp157s0np0`, HCA prefix `ibp`). The residual footgun (an FFI all-gather under an explicit mesh) was filed upstream as [jax-ml/jax#38551](https://github.com/jax-ml/jax/issues/38551) via [#6470](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6470).

## 3. What didn't work (the blockers)

- **First-step memory wall.** ~40 diagnostic runs (MAY-020…MAY-060) localized a deterministic pre-step OOM: full-depth d2560/26-layer, seq-4096, batch-256 runs reliably requested **~63–74 GiB before step 0**, un-rescuable by fp32-vs-bf16 master params, Pallas-vs-XLA cross-entropy, or even `recompute_all` remat (which only trimmed the request to ~72 GiB / got to ~91→90 GiB before OOMing on a 27 GiB request). Batch-128 fit, one-layer seq-4096 fit, and batch-256 at seq-2048 fit — pinning it to full-depth activation/remat liveness. 128- and 256-device launches also hit `Initialize clique` rendezvous failures before any step ([#6367](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367)).
- **Muon is structurally too expensive on H100.** Single-node profiling ([#6493](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6493)) found **MuonH at 1.49 s/step (~22K tok/s, ~4.75% MFU) vs an SGD A/B at 0.44 s/step (~75K tok/s, ~16.2% MFU)** on the identical shape — ~1.05 s/step of pure Muon overhead. The root cause is not the NS compute but the *sharding*: Newton-Schulz wants whole matrices local, so redistributing updates back into the FSDP weight layout is an **expert-weight-sized ~121.9 GiB bf16 payload that does not shrink with node count and is hard to overlap** — roughly doubling the un-hideable all-gather. On a 2-node run the Muon tax was **+0.92 s/step (~60% throughput hit), growing from +0.76 s at one node** as the grad all-gather crossed the inter-node link ([#6532](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6532)); a 2-node dashboard put Muon's NS work at **~500 ms of a 1,500 ms step** ([June 22-28 digest](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1521459161844416553/1521459703782178986)). **Verdict (June 26): shelve Muon on GPU** — dlwh, #gpu: *"We're currently at ~19.9 MFU on 4 nodes w/ SGD/Adam… Muon needs to go for now. 10% step-count improvement isn't worth [it]… We'll need much faster interconnect or PP"* ([discord](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1399998407657001062/1520200189690773564)). Accordingly, in the #6693 tracker the MuonH row is `SKIP`/`No muon` — the bar was being chased on the SGD/AdamH path, not the production optimizer.
- **June pipeline-parallelism prototype couldn't beat FSDP — parked.** Russell Power's manual-threaded 1F1B / zero-bubble PP ([#6532](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6532), PR [#6534](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/pull/6534)) was gradient-exact and *won on TPU* (v6e-32, ~6.91B grug-MoE, 4-way PP × 4 EP × 2 data — "pipeline parallelism beats FSDP at multi-host scale"). On GPU the ring EP lowered/executed (the "make-or-break PASSED on 8×H100"), but throughput never beat FSDP: single-node was overhead-bound, and 2-node on fast IB reached only **2.62× FSDP (i.e., still ~2.6× slower)**, root-caused to a *structural* per-op `set_mesh` dispatch bound that "can't be hoisted or dropped." **Close-out June 25: parked — moved out of marin to rjpower/marin-experiments#11, PR #6534 closed unmerged.** (Note: this hand-rolled driver is a *different* PP vehicle from July's JaxPP, §7.)
- **DeepEP never beat ring in a real train step.** Even the best DeepEP variant lost to ring EP: an assignment-native DeepEP path recovered a chunk of the gap (**~19.7% MFU vs ~16% on EP16, achieved**) but was never wired into a jitted Grug train step and never measured against ring at train time; ring stayed the fastest measured EP16/N2 backend, with ragged / padded / assigned-token all-to-all variants all losing to it ([#4312](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4312)).

## 4. The 128-GPU bar was never cleared on H100

The bar ([#6693](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6693)) is **">20 MFU at 128 GPUs"**, and no row reached it in-corpus. The 128-GPU attempts were failures, not measurements: MAY-034 (128 devices) died on repeated `Initialize clique` rendezvous; MAY-060 (128-GPU B128 full-seq, launched June 16) only ever reported `JOB_STATE_RUNNING` before the thread pivoted to FA4/CuTe attention. #6367's own TL;DR: *"No ≥20 MFU claim yet."* So the load-bearing **~19.9% is at 32 GPUs (4 nodes), on SGD/Adam — a quarter of the bar's scale and not the production optimizer.** It does not project over the bar: cross-node scaling *loses* MFU (the optimizer comms "do not scale away with node count"), and the production MuonH path is shelved on GPU.

## 5. The itemized roofline — "what worked / what each thing cost" ([#6979](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6979), via [July 6-12 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-07-06_2026-07-12.html))

The single most definitive attribution is Larry Dial's "GPU MFU Learning Path," which builds the Hopper story from scratch on a single 8×H100 node (all figures achieved on that node unless noted). *(#6979/#6998/#7012/#7024 post-date the corpus freeze as GitHub threads and return "not found"; the numbers below are attested in-corpus only via the frozen July 6-12 weekly summary, which narrates #6979 in full.)*

| Stage (single 8×H100 node) | MFU | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| lm_head-only cross-entropy bench | **50.3%** | Liger chunked CE, one chunk/device — a *ceiling*, not trainable |
| Full **dense** d2560 / 26-layer model | **56.2%** | FA4 CUTLASS, ZeRO-1, scan-over-layers + full remat — dense *ceiling*, no MoE correctness |
| Throughput probe → full trainable `model.py` | **34.4% → 26.7%** | itemized give-back as each MoE correctness feature is switched on |

The give-back ladder: **real Muon's Newton-Schulz orthogonalization is the single biggest give-back at ~3.7 points**; **each norm only ~0.4–1 point** (the remaining line-items between 34.4% and 26.7% are itemized only inside #6979 and are not recoverable from the frozen corpus). An earlier step-10 OOM was diagnosed as a false ceiling — the per-parameter-norm *watch callback* unstacking all 26 scanned layers, not a real memory wall.

Two in-corpus precursor profiles feed this (do **not** conflate them — they are separate runs):
- **MAY-042 ([#6367](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6367)):** a **16-node (128-GPU), B128, seq-4096** profile — **~65.7% compute / 33.7% communication / 0.7% stall**, top compute op = FA4/CuTe segmented-attention *backward*, top collective = NCCL all-gather. **No MFU claimed** (never recovered steady-state step markers).
- **May215 ([#4312](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/4312)):** a **single-node, B8** SGD profile at **~16.2% MFU** with device-time split **MoE 43% / XLA CE 17% / FA4 flash 10% / collectives 5%** — showing the cost is *not* just expert matmul (ring-EP transport/permute/combine and the ~17% CE are comparably hot).

**Multinode weak-scaling (the #6979 headline):** the trainable `model.py` weak-scales essentially **flat — 26.7% at 8 GPUs → 26.5% at 64 H100 (achieved), global batch 1024** — because replica/DDP hides the once-per-step gradient all-reduce over the datacenter network while keeping the frequent FSDP all-gathers on intra-node NVLink; **sharding the model across nodes instead costs ~4 points**. This required **dropping total experts 256 → 64 (still top-4)** so the model fits one node — the "H100 working copy" (~18B total params, a quarter of the 67B/256-expert production shape). Larry's `dial-h100-fullmodelpy-d2560-e64-*` wandb runs (2026-07-07) are this working copy (`parameter_count ≈ 18B`); most are `state=failed`/`crashed` and none logs an `mfu` field — the 26.5% was profiled and reported in #6979, not captured in a wandb scalar. So "no clean full-shape H100 training MFU in wandb" is true, but "no H100 MFU exists" is wrong.

**Reading the "final MFU":** the **67B/256-expert shape never trained on H100.** The closest production-shape H100 number is **~19.9% (achieved, 32 GPU, SGD/Adam) — below its own 20% bar, at ¼ the bar's 128-GPU scale, on a substitute optimizer.** The higher 23.8% (a smaller d2048 model) and 26.5% (the reduced 64-expert working copy) are **not the 67B-A2B**. ## 7. How it resolved: the run went to TPU; H100 was re-scoped and revived

**The hero run moved to TPU.** After the H100 stall, Larry Dial staged the d2560 shape onto TPU v4 (where XLA handles Muon's Newton-Schulz cheaply — the NS step is ~0.9% of step time, vs ~33% on H100). It started on a v4-1024 the morning of June 24; the **v4-1024 EP=1 run measured 21.54% MFU (achieved, per-chip best, crashed step 4367) with a ~78-day 10T projection** ([#6044](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6044)). The team then **deliberately chose the full v4-2048 (1,024 chips), accepting *lower* per-chip MFU for absolute throughput and a ~45-day wall-clock**, launching `moe_67b_a2b_d2560_ep1_rep16_bs4096_seq8192_sw2k_v4_2048_muon_10T` on the May Recipe with MuonH. hammer confirmed the pivot: *"you mentioned we have changed plans for this run and now plan to run on TPUs instead of GPUs."* By July it was ~2.1T tokens in (step 39,000, ~21% of 10.07T), **holding MFU near ~18.6% (achieved) on the resumed `resume15k_v2` run** — the run's *final* sustained MFU figure — with the 2T intermediate cooldown landing at **2.277 Paloma** (favorable vs the preregistered 2.269 stage-1 target), completion targeted ~Aug 30 ([July 6-12 summary](https://mws.oa.dev/summaries/summary-2026-07-06_2026-07-12.html), [#6811](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6811), epic [#6704](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6704)). *(So: 21.54% is the v4-1024 per-chip best; ~18.6% is the actual sustained number on the launched v4-2048 run.)*

**Hopper was then explicitly re-opened, not abandoned.** In July, David Hall opened a **GPU Upskilling Master Plan ([#6998](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6998))** naming the Hopper obstacles — *"high sparsity plus Adam taxes HBM, the MoE block is slow, and Muon carries high overhead at high sparsity, with pipeline parallelism the real fix"* — and set an explicit **XXB-A2B-on-Hopper target of 20–25% MFU on 128–256 H100 (target)**. Two threads advanced the "real fix":
- **Fused EP kernel ([#6597](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6597)):** a SonicMoE-style kernel extended for EP over NVLink, *"showing promise at getting genuine overlap with the all-gathers where nothing else had."* By the July close-out it completed the exact EP8 target forward+backward through one JAX-native custom VJP (integrated fwd+bwd ~242.8 ms, ~31.9 TFLOP/s per rank) — promising, in-flight, not yet a delivered target-shape MFU.
- **JaxPP pipeline parallelism ([#7024](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7024)):** a *real* PP library (unlike June's hand-rolled dispatch-bound driver). On July 10 David Hall flagged it *"putting Hopper back on the menu."* Its **best measured point reached 18.26% mean MFU (achieved, 32 H100, ~1.74 pts below the 20% bar)** after a disciplined week of negative results (latency-hiding flag +0.4%; XLA output-oriented ring combine −52%; transfer-priority ordering −2.9%; input-grad-first backward −29%; two-chunk bulk ring −14%). The most promising lever — an EP-local QuACK/Sonic grouped-MLP adapter — cleared the perf gate (1.16× fwd) but failed output parity (~2.6% mismatch traced to QuACK's fused approximate SwiGLU); a concurrent-QuACK FSDP-3 hang was fixed with a per-handler mutex validated at 32-H100, though at 13.96 MFU it is not itself competitive with ring EP.

So the close-out state is an **active, re-scoped Hopper push — real in direction and tooling, still short of the 20% bar in achieved numbers** — not "we gave up on H100."

**What the H100 upskilling feeds (two distinct Blackwell runs, not one):** (a) an **~Aug-1 ~120B-A8B on 2 NVL72 B200**, gated on B200 MFU clearing 20% — currently **17.8% (achieved, single-node 8×B200)** via Tri Dao's QuACK/SonicMoE kernel ([#7012](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7012), [#6706](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6706), [#6710](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6710)); and (b) an **~Sept-15 ~512B-A16B on 20T tokens on 12 NVL72**, targeting 25% MFU. The near-term 20% B200 gate is what the H100 numbers most directly serve.

## Gaps / caveats

- **#6979, #6998, #7012, #7024 post-date the freeze as GitHub threads** (`show` returns "not found in corpus"); their numbers (50.3 / 56.2 / 34.4 / 26.7 / 26.5%, the ~3.7-pt Muon give-back, the ~4-pt cross-node cost, JaxPP 18.26%, B200 17.8%, the 20–25% target) are attested in-corpus **only via the frozen July 6-12 weekly summary narrative**. They are internally consistent with in-corpus wandb metadata (param counts, watch-callback OOM, nowatch/replica/fsdp variants) but not primary-source-verifiable here.
- **Muon's cost is stage-dependent:** ~11.4 MFU-pts / ~1.05 s-step *raw* (June, [#6493](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6493), 4.75% vs 16.2%) vs the ~3.7-pt *residual* give-back after the grouped/4D-NS optimization (July, #6979). Cite the stage or it reads as contradictory.
- **Roofline vs delivered:** 56.2% (dense) and 50.3% (lm_head-only) are *ceilings*, not trainable-MoE MFU; the trainable deliverable is 26.7% (8 H100) / 26.5% (64 H100).
- A separate **67B-A2B *inference* / export path on GPUs** landed mid-July ([#7116](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7116) merged; [#7117](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/7117) open), with an 8×H100 BF16 rerun hitting an NCCL AllToAll error — that is serving/export, not the training bring-up this question is about.
- **#4302 was closed as done/superseded** by the current GPU MFU threads (July); it is the historical framing issue, not the live tracker.

### Same-pool corroboration of the pure-FSDP ceiling (90B sibling — not the 67B shape)

The sibling **90B-total / 5.3B-active** Grug MoE on the same CoreWeave 256-H100 pool drove **2.8% → 9.37% MFU** (3.3×, batch 1024 × seq 2048 + save_moe, ~712K tok/s), which rjpower closed as *"the practical pure-FSDP ceiling for this 90B/5.3BA model"* ([#6304](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6304), levers follow-up [#6321](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6321); batch 1536/2048, PGLE, reshard-fix, pure-bf16 params, collective-combine, and microbatching all OOMed or regressed). A second independent witness on the same hardware that pure-FSDP all-gather comms cap MoE MFU far below the ~25% target — distinct from the 67B-A2B shape (67B total / 2.01B active).
